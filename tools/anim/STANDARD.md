# The animation standard

One table, every rule with a unique id. Generated from `rules.json` (`python authoring/checks/rules.py --markdown`); the rows and the checks cannot drift apart:
`build.sh check --only rules` fails a gate rule whose item or limit does not exist, and `draftcheck.py` raises an error for an item that has no row.

Terms (owner, 2026-11-24): **inactive** stance = the gun lowered (game Safe); **active** stance = the gun raised. A reload variant starts and ends in the idle of the stance it plays in;
only the discard rise is added. Poses never change for a reload.

How a rule is enforced:
- **gate**: a check item that runs in `build.sh check` (areas `rules`, `draft`, `lowvar`, `pose`, `lowered`, `raised`, ...), in `ga produce` (its check step) and in `ga drafts` (draftcheck on every draft; the command exits 1 when a draft fails a rule). A clip that fails a gate rule fails the command. There are no baselines and no per-gun exceptions.
- **owner-eye**: no measure ranks it; the owner judges at pick time (real-speed video).
- **not yet enforceable**: measured, no limit separates the owner's approved from rejected clips; the reason is in the row.

Calibration principle (coordinator 2026-11-24): owner-approved clips are the reference set and must pass (the 24 approved Warden variants, the SV13/SV14 finals, shipped Warden ST32B/ST38B/ST40B where they are the clips that play); owner-rejected ones
(round 5 ST55B, ST58B, ST59B, ST63B; round 4 ST44 to ST53, too slow) must fail. Where a rule cannot do both, the conflict is listed below, not loosened silently.

Limits built on the Warden mesh (R7 finger torque, R11 two-gun clearance, R14 hidden phase and spin) are Warden-only: they gate no other gun until a limit is measured on that gun's own approved clips (the generic discard layer `reload/discard.py` can measure other guns; it has no approved reference for them).

## Discard-visible variants (R9, R18)

Owner 2026-11-24: showing the discard is not required for every reload, and never for very large guns (lifting them into view does not look natural). A variant is made only where the lift looks natural, measured from the approved Warden variants: offset at most 46.5 cm and pitch at most 23.8 deg (approved max x 1.1), and the gun's screen cover in the raised game pose at most 12.4 % (Warden 11.3 x 1.1; Masamune 15.0 is too big, Warden is fine; the lift alone would allow Masamune at 21.7 cm). Above a limit no variant is made, the shipped clip stays in that stance and R9 does not apply. R9 gates only the variants that are made. The rule covers every gun, including the halted LMG, precision and sniper work (untouched). Per gun and stance: `VARIANTS.md`.
